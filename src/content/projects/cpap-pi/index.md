Infants and children on CPAP in the NICU and PICU wear a tight mask for long stretches, and the skin under it can break down. This project, with W. Hong Yeo’s group, builds a mask that detects pressure injury.

Xinye designed the multi-sensor Bluetooth Low Energy board in the mask and the tablet app that reads it at the bedside. The prototype was demoed at Children’s Healthcare of Atlanta in August 2026.
