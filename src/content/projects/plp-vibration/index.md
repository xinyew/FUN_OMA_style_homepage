This project reviews the vibration settings used in earlier studies of phantom limb pain (frequency, amplitude, site and pattern) and builds a wearable to test them.

The device places five eccentric-rotating-mass motors evenly on a strap that wraps the residual limb, driven by an ESP32-C3. The study is with people who have below-knee amputations; one question it asks is whether a smaller vibration dose still helps.
