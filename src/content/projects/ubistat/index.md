UbiStat is a miniaturized wireless platform for ubiquitous electrochemical sensing. Electrochemical sensors can read proteins, ions and metabolites, but the instruments that drive them stay on lab benches. UbiStat puts the measurement front end, the radio and the power budget on one coin-cell board for about $50 in parts, and reads four electrodes once an hour for roughly forty days.

The board began as the electronics for an implantable sensor that tracks four cancer-related proteins at once. As a platform it now points further: distributed water-quality sensing in Hawaiian taro fields and non-invasive sweat cortisol sensing are being explored, each waiting on a mature front-end sensor.
